How a drive works

From planning to the plate.

Identify

Volunteers find communities in need: slums, shelters, orphanages and roadside families.

Collect

Food, ration kits and clothing are gathered through donations and surplus-food collection.

Distribute

Volunteers serve meals and hand out clothes and kits directly to people.

Share

Each drive is recorded and shared so supporters can see where help went.

Questions

Things people often ask.

Who runs Project Seva?

Project Seva is run by InAmigos Foundation, a Section 8 non-profit company founded on 23 September 2020 and based in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh.

Who receives help?

People in slum areas, orphanages and low-income families, as well as homeless individuals who need food or warm clothing.

Can I donate clothes instead of money?

Clothing support is part of Seva. Contact the foundation to arrange a clothes donation.

Is my donation tax deductible?

The foundation states it holds 80G certification. Always ask for an official 80G receipt when you donate.

How do I contact the team?
