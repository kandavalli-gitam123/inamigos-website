How a drive works

From planning to a cleaner, greener place.

Choose

Volunteers pick a spot that needs trees or a clean-up: a roadside, park or village area.

Gather

Saplings, gloves, bags and bins are arranged, and local people are invited to join.

Act

Teams plant saplings, clear litter and set up waste segregation points.

Nurture

Saplings are watered and protected so they grow into healthy trees.

Questions

Things people often ask.

Who runs Project Prakriti?

Project Prakriti is run by InAmigos Foundation, a Section 8 non-profit company founded on 23 September 2020 and based in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh.

What kind of trees are planted?

Native trees suited to the local climate are the best choice, as they grow well and support local birds and insects. Ask the team about the saplings used in your area.

Can I plant a tree on my own?

Yes. Pick a native sapling, plant it where it has room to grow, and water it regularly for the first couple of years. Share a photo with the foundation to be part of the movement.

Can my school or college host a drive?

Yes. Contact the foundation to plan a tree-planting or clean-up drive with your group.
