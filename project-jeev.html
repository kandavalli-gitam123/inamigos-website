Questions

Things people often ask.

Who runs Project Jeev?

Project Jeev is run by InAmigos Foundation, a Section 8 non-profit company founded on 23 September 2020 and based in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh.

Which animals does Jeev help?

Mainly street dogs and cats, along with other stray animals that need food, water or care.

What should I feed a street dog?

Plain cooked rice with curd, boiled eggs, or plain chapati are common safe options. Avoid chocolate, onions, grapes, very salty or spicy food, and cooked bones.

Can I join a feeding round?

Yes. Contact the foundation to find out about feeding activities near you, or start by feeding animals on your own street.
