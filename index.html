Who we are

InAmigos Foundation was founded on 23 September 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla (Founder & CEO). It is a not-for-profit company registered under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013, with its base in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh.

We believe in the power of collective action. Through a network of volunteers, interns and partners, every contribution goes towards food distribution, education, women's empowerment, animal welfare, environmental sustainability and skill development across India.

COVID-19 relief

Within months of starting, the foundation stepped up during the pandemic.

🍛Distributed food to 1,500+ people during COVID-19.
✏️Distributed stationery to students in Kolkata.
👕Distributed clothes across many regions of Chhattisgarh.
😷Distributed masks and sanitiser in rural areas.
🐕Fed street animals through the pandemic.
