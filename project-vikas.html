    Questions

    Things people often ask.

    Who runs Project Vikas?

    Project Vikas is run by InAmigos Foundation, a Section 8 non-profit company founded on 23 September 2020 and based in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh.

    Who is Vikas for?

    Young people and adults who want to improve their skills and chances of finding work, especially those from underprivileged backgrounds.

    Are the internships paid?

    Terms vary by role. Check the stipend, duration and any targets in each listing before you apply, and ask the team if anything is unclear.

    Can I volunteer as a trainer or mentor?

    Yes. If you have professional skills to share, contact the foundation about running a session or mentoring participants.

    How do I contact the team?

    Email inamigosfoundation@gmail.com or call +91 626 730 9902.
