How it works

From finding a child to keeping them in class.

Reach out

Volunteers visit rural areas and communities to find children who are out of school or struggling.

Equip

Children receive books, stationery, uniforms and other essentials they need to study.

Teach

Community classes, often held outdoors, help children catch up and keep learning.

Support

Where possible, help with school fees keeps children enrolled for the long term.

Questions

Things people often ask.

Who runs Project Bachpanshala?

Project Bachpanshala is run by InAmigos Foundation, a Section 8 non-profit company founded on 23 September 2020 and based in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh.

Which children does it help?

Children in rural and underprivileged areas who cannot attend school or keep up with their studies because of economic or social barriers.

Can I volunteer as a teacher?

Yes. The foundation offers a Community Teacher volunteer role. Contact the team to find out about current openings.

Can I donate books or stationery?

School supplies are a core part of Bachpanshala. Contact the foundation to arrange a donation of books, stationery or uniforms.

How do I contact the team?

Email inamigosfoundation@gmail.com or call +91 626 730 9902.
