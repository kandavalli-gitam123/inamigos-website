Questions

Things people often ask.

Who runs Project Udaan?

Project Udaan is run by InAmigos Foundation, a Section 8 non-profit company founded on 23 September 2020 and based in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh.

Who can take part?

Women and girls from underprivileged backgrounds who want to learn new skills and move toward financial independence.

What are the digital campaigns?

Online awareness campaigns on topics such as women's rights, health and opportunities, shared through social media to reach girls across India.

Can I volunteer as a trainer or mentor?

Yes. If you have a skill to share, from tailoring to spreadsheets, contact the foundation about volunteering with Udaan.

How do I contact the team?
